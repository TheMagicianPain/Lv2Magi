Questions You May Have

Before you decide.

What if I don't know who I'm becoming?

You don't need a perfectly defined identity before starting. Part of the work is discovering what becomes clearer when you stop organizing your life around identities that no longer fit.

What if 30 days isn't enough?

Thirty days isn't meant to finish your transformation. It's a focused initiation: clarify the direction, begin the experiment, create evidence, and establish momentum you can continue after the container ends.

What if I already know what I want?

Then we can spend less time on discovery and more time on expression and execution—turning what you already know into decisions, work, and action.

Is this therapy?

No. Private Initiation is coaching and personal development focused on identity, expression, decisions, and execution. It is not a substitute for licensed mental-health treatment.

What if I don't have a personal brand?

You don't need one. Personal branding is only one possible arena. The work can apply to your career, art, relationships, appearance, lifestyle, or any other area where you want to express yourself more deliberately.

What if I'm a musician or artist?

That's absolutely within the scope. Bring the music, art, ideas, or unfinished work you're trying to express. The goal is not to turn you into a particular kind of artist; it's to help your work become more aligned with the person you're becoming.

What if I don't want to be an influencer?

You don't have to be. Public expression does not mean becoming an influencer. This is about living and expressing your chosen identity more deliberately, whether that happens online or privately.

Why do I need direct access to you?

Because implementation creates questions that a static course can't answer. Direct access gives you a place to bring decisions, resistance, work-in-progress, and real situations while you're actually changing.

Why does it cost $888?

Private Initiation is deliberately limited to a small number of people because it includes private 1:1 work, 30 days of direct access, and a personal implementation container rather than a self-guided product.

Why is there a $4.44 discovery call?

The call is intentionally a seriousness filter. The $4.44 fee is non-refundable and is not credited toward the $888 Private Initiation.
